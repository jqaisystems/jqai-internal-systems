# AI Brief Generator

Case study only. No source code, credentials, prompts, form submissions, logs, or private client data are included.

Public page: [AI Brief Generator](https://www.ai.joaoqueiros.com/systems/ai-brief-generator)

## Problem

Creative briefs often start from vague intake answers and require a long discovery call plus several hours of writing before they become useful.

## System Pattern

The system turns a short intake form into a structured draft brief covering audience, brand tone, competitive context, visual direction, and scope notes. A human reviews and edits before sending anything to a client.

## Stack

Claude API, PHP or Python workflow layer, structured intake form, branded export, human review.

## Outcome

The studio gets from raw intake to a useful first brief much faster, while preserving expert judgment in the final edit.

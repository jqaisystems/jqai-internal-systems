# Social Media Content Calendar

Case study only. No source code, credentials, prompts, client calendars, logs, or private brand documents are included.

Public page: [Social Media Content Calendar](https://www.ai.joaoqueiros.com/systems/content-calendar)

## Problem

Planning a month of content requires translating brand strategy into platform-specific ideas, captions, posting rhythm, and reusable themes. The first draft takes too long when done manually.

## What The System Does

The system reads a brand brief, generates a month of content ideas, adapts them by platform, and produces a structured calendar for review.

## Workflow

1. Start from a brand brief or campaign direction.
2. Generate content pillars and recurring themes.
3. Draft platform-specific post ideas and captions.
4. Organize the month into a calendar structure.
5. Review, edit, and approve before publishing.

## Outcome

The first draft of a content calendar becomes a short generation and review step instead of a blank-page planning session.

Public website outcome: 30 days of content planned in under 10 minutes, with 60+ platform-ready post ideas per run.

## What Can Be Adapted For Clients

- Monthly content calendars for agencies and creators.
- Platform-specific caption workflows.
- Campaign planning assistants.
- Editorial systems that keep human review before publishing.

## Problem

Finding good prospects is only the first step. The time disappears in enrichment, qualification, deciding who is worth contacting, drafting a relevant first message, and tracking the next action.

## What The System Does

OutreachIQ combines search, enrichment, AI scoring, personalized draft generation, and pipeline tracking in one dashboard. The human reviews each lead, message, and next step before outreach happens.

## Workflow

1. Search for candidate companies or contacts by market, location, or niche.
2. Enrich each lead with public website and business context.
3. Score the lead against a consistent qualification rubric.
4. Generate a first outreach draft using the lead context.
5. Review, edit, export, or move the lead to the next step.
6. Keep the pipeline state visible in one place.

## Outcome

The system compresses multi-day manual prospecting into a structured review workflow. It improves consistency by applying the same scoring rubric and drafting pattern to every lead.

Public website outcome: 3 days of manual prospecting compressed to under 1 hour, with 50+ scored leads per run.

## What Can Be Adapted For Clients

- Lead qualification dashboards.
- Market research and enrichment pipelines.
- Human-reviewed outreach systems.
- Sales ops workflows where AI supports prioritization but does not send messages without approval.

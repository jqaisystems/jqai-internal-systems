# SEO Engine

[Website case study](https://www.ai.joaoqueiros.com/systems/seo-engine) · [System film](https://www.ai.joaoqueiros.com/media/systems/films/seo-engine.mp4)

Status: internal search-operations workflow for the JQ AI SYSTEMS website. Public breakdown dated 14 October 2026.

## Problem

As a publishing site grows, checking indexing, dead links and the effect of content changes becomes an operations task. A traffic chart alone does not identify which action to take next.

## Workflow

1. Pull search and traffic evidence from Search Console and GA4.
2. Inspect sitemap URLs for coverage, crawl and canonical status.
3. Generate the sitemap with checks for missing pages.
4. Check public URL responses after an upload.
5. Notify supported search engines through IndexNow; use the sitemap and selective manual indexing requests for Google.
6. Compare equal reporting windows at dated checkpoints, applying decision rules chosen before reviewing results.

## What changes

Indexing, sitemap integrity and search experiments become a repeatable routine. Dated checkpoints distinguish a persistent change from a temporary topic spike.

Traffic is not equivalent to client enquiries. Search results depend on content, competition and changing demand; this case study makes no ranking or growth guarantee.

## Public evidence

The website case study contains the dated measurements and their caveats. The film illustrates the operating routine. This repository does not mirror private analytics or imply that the film is a live dashboard.

## Adaptable patterns

Publishing checks, URL-response verification, indexing reviews and evidence-based search experiments.

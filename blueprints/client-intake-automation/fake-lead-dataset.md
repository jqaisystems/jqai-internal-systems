## Demo Uses

- Test whether the rubric separates strong-fit leads from vague inquiries.
- Populate the review queue without using real data.
- Demonstrate how different recommended actions change the first-response draft.
- Show that the system can say "ask follow-up" or "decline" instead of pushing every inquiry into a call.

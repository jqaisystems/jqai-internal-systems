Case study only. No source code, credentials, prompts, databases, logs, or API keys are included.

Public page: [Etsy to Pinterest Pipeline](https://www.ai.joaoqueiros.com/systems/etsy-to-pinterest)  
Public demo: [guided walkthrough](https://www.ai.joaoqueiros.com/demo/etsy-to-pinterest.html)

## Problem

Every new Etsy listing needs a Pinterest-ready image, title, description, alt text, board choice, and posting time. The work is small but constant, and skipping it weakens product discovery.

## What The System Does

The system imports listing context, prepares pin assets, detects product niche, drafts Pinterest SEO metadata, deduplicates repeated assets, and sends approved pins into a review and scheduling flow.

## Workflow

1. Bring listing data into a dashboard.
2. Prepare or select the best product image for Pinterest.
3. Generate title, description, alt text, keywords, board suggestions, and posting notes.
4. Check for duplicate or repeated assets.
5. Let a human review and approve.
6. Send approved pins to Pinterest directly or through an automation bridge.

## Outcome

The workflow changes daily manual cross-posting into a short review step. Pinterest output becomes more consistent while the human operator still controls what gets published.

Public website outcome: daily cross-posting reduced from roughly 60 minutes to a short review flow.

## What Can Be Adapted For Clients

- Marketplace-to-social promotion workflows.
- Product SEO metadata generation.
- Pinterest, Instagram, or catalog posting pipelines.
- Review queues for ecommerce teams that want AI support without auto-publishing blindly.

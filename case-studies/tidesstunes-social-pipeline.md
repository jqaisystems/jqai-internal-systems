## Problem

Long-form music videos need to become short vertical clips, each with platform-specific captions, hashtags, scheduling, and status tracking. The work is repetitive enough to delay publishing, especially when the studio is busy.

## What The System Does

The pipeline turns source videos into social-ready assets, prepares captions for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and Pinterest, routes them through a review step, then sends approved posts into a scheduling workflow.

## Workflow

1. Collect source videos and campaign context.
2. Generate short 9:16 clip candidates.
3. Prepare platform-specific captions and hashtags.
4. Store assets in a review queue.
5. Push approved posts to the scheduler.
6. Track queue state so the studio knows what is ready, scheduled, or already published.

## Outcome

The system converts a manual distribution routine into a repeatable content pipeline. A batch of music videos can become a queue of social posts without rebuilding the workflow each time.

Public website outcome: 17 videos processed and 39+ posts queued.

## What Can Be Adapted For Clients

- YouTube-to-short-form content pipelines.
- Review-first social publishing workflows.
- Multi-platform caption generation.
- Content queues for small teams that need consistency without hiring a dedicated operator.

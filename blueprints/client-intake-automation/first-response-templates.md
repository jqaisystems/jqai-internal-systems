# First Response Templates

These are draft-only templates. The system should prepare them for review, not send them automatically.

## Strong Fit: Discovery Call

**Use when:** The request matches the offer, the problem is clear, and the timeline is reasonable.

```text
Hi [First name],

Thanks for sharing the details. Based on what you described, this looks like a good fit for an intake and follow-up automation workflow.

The main opportunity I see is to reduce manual triage, make each inquiry easier to review, and prepare a first response without removing human approval.

The next useful step would be a short discovery call so I can understand the current workflow, the tools involved, and where the review step should sit.

Best,
[Your name]
```

## Likely Fit: Follow-Up Questions

**Use when:** The request seems relevant but one or two key details are missing.

```text
Hi [First name],

Thanks for reaching out. This may be a fit, but I would want to clarify a few details before suggesting the right next step:

1. Where do new inquiries currently arrive?
2. What happens after a good inquiry comes in?
3. Which part is most painful right now: qualification, response writing, follow-up, or logging?
4. Do you already have a preferred place to track leads?

Once I have that context, I can tell you whether this should be a simple intake workflow or a larger client operations system.

Best,
[Your name]
```

## Unclear Fit: Context Request

**Use when:** The prospect is interested in AI but has not named a specific workflow.

```text
Hi [First name],

Thanks for the note. Before recommending anything, I would want to narrow this down to one concrete workflow.

The kinds of projects that tend to work best are repetitive, reviewable processes like intake triage, reporting, content preparation, or follow-up reminders.

If you can send one example of a task your team repeats every week, I can help decide whether it is a good candidate for automation.

Best,
[Your name]
```

## Weak Fit: Polite Redirect

**Use when:** The request is outside scope or too large for the current offer.

```text
Hi [First name],

Thanks for reaching out. Based on the current request, I do not think this is the right fit for the kind of workflow systems I build.

My work is focused on focused operational automations with human review steps, rather than broad software builds or full platform replacements.

If your needs become more focused around intake, reporting, follow-up, or review queues, feel free to reach out again with that specific workflow.

Best,
[Your name]
```

## Reviewer Checklist

- Does the draft match the actual offer?
- Does it avoid overpromising?
- Does it make the next step clear?
- Does it sound personal enough for the inquiry?
- Is it safe to send after replacing placeholders?

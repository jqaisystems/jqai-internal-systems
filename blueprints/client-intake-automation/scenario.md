# Scenario

## Target User

A solo consultant, small studio, or service team that receives new business inquiries but does not have a dedicated operations person to qualify every lead.

The best-fit user already has a clear service offer, but the first few messages with prospects are still repetitive: clarifying scope, budget, timeline, fit, and next steps.

## Default Demo Scenario

A small AI automation studio receives a new inquiry from a potential client who wants help improving their intake, reporting, or follow-up workflow.

The inquiry arrives through a short website form. The system turns the submission into a structured lead summary, assigns a fit score, drafts a recommended first response, and waits for a human reviewer to approve, edit, hold, or reject it.

## Trigger

A new form submission is received.

## Inputs

- Service need.
- Business type.
- Current workflow pain.
- Timeline.
- Budget clarity.
- Existing tools.
- Preferred next step.
- Free-form context.

## Outputs

- Reviewer lead summary.
- Fit score.
- Missing context flags.
- Recommended next action.
- Draft first response.
- Review queue item.

## Success Metrics

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| First triage time | Under 5 minutes from form submission to review-ready summary. |
| Follow-up clarity | Reviewer can see the main missing questions without rereading the full inquiry. |
| Response consistency | Similar inquiries receive similar next-step recommendations. |
| Human control | No outbound response is sent without approval. |

## Non-Goals

- Replacing a sales conversation.
- Auto-sending replies without review.
- Storing sensitive submission details in public demo assets.
- Predicting exact deal value.
- Making qualification decisions that cannot be overridden by the owner.

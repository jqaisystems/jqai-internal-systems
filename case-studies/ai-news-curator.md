## Problem

AI, coding, design, and automation signals move quickly across newsletters, social feeds, GitHub, communities, and saved links. Useful material can disappear into chat threads, browser tabs, or private notes before it becomes a reusable briefing, content idea, or product decision.

## What The System Does

AI News Curator collects and organizes saved links and source signals into a private command center. It supports bulk link import, source-specific ingestion, public/private visibility, archive grouping by day and month, and daily brief generation for human review.

## Workflow

1. Import saved links or ingest approved public sources.
2. Normalize each item into a consistent saved-link record.
3. Classify items by source, topic, date, and visibility.
4. Group the archive by local day, month, and year.
5. Generate a daily briefing from the latest relevant items.
6. Review the brief, keep useful links, and decide what becomes public, private, or archived.
7. Use selected signals for research, content planning, client context, or internal product decisions.

## Outcome

The system turns scattered reading into a repeatable signal workflow. Instead of losing links across tabs and messages, the operator can review a structured archive and daily briefing while keeping private notes and saved links out of public channels by default.

Public website: [AI News Curator](https://www.ai.joaoqueiros.com/systems/ai-news-curator).

## What Can Be Adapted For Clients

- Research briefing dashboards for founders, consultants, and small teams.
- Market-intelligence feeds for specific niches or competitors.
- Content research queues that separate private notes from public-ready material.
- Source-monitoring workflows with human-reviewed daily or weekly summaries.
- Internal knowledge hubs for teams tracking fast-moving technology changes.

# Client Portal Automation

Case study only. No source code, credentials, prompts, client files, logs, or private project data are included.

Public page: [Client Portal Automation](https://www.ai.joaoqueiros.com/systems/client-portal)

## Problem

Client onboarding, weekly updates, deliverable summaries, and handoff notes are necessary but repetitive. Solo consultants and small studios often delay them when production work gets busy.

## System Pattern

The system is designed to automate onboarding reminders, summarize project progress, and prepare client-facing status updates from structured project notes.

## Stack

PHP, Claude API, email workflow, project-status summaries, human approval before client delivery.

## Outcome

The goal is to reduce client management overhead while keeping communication personal and reviewed.
